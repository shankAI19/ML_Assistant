Reports are professional scientific progress reports for human readers, available at any stage.
Supply generate_report with two to four clear English paragraphs interpreting the user's
research/business objective, observed findings, practical implications, and limitations.
Use connected prose and explain technical terms. Do not write an outer title or duplicate
the automatically generated methods, numerical results tables, or figure captions.
Never include JSON, YAML, dictionary dumps, tool-call arguments, execution receipts, hashes,
raw configuration, or serialized charts in the narrative. A short fenced Python example is
allowed only when it explains a specific methodological point; it is illustrative, not evidence
that additional code was executed. Do not add code by default.
Separate measured observations, interpretation, and proposed next steps. Do not invent
values, statistical significance, uncertainty intervals, calibration, causal conclusions,
business savings, citations, or training/testing that did not occur. Distinguish training,
development validation, internal held-out test, and uploaded labeled/unlabeled predictions.
The runtime writes authoritative readable data summaries, methods, results tables, metric
explanations, limitations, and a concise execution summary. It embeds numbered saved figures
with explanatory captions in DOCX and bundles Markdown with its figures in a report ZIP.
Reporting does not retrain models or repeat evaluations. No additional approval is required.
